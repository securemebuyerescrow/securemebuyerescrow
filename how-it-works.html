How SecureMeBuyerEscrow Works

Step 1: Buyer and Seller Agree

Both parties agree to the terms of the transaction and initiate the escrow process.

Step 2: Buyer Pays Escrow

The buyer deposits funds into the secure escrow account.

Step 3: Seller Delivers Domain

The seller transfers the domain to the buyer.

Step 4: Funds Released

Once the buyer confirms receipt, the funds are released to the seller.
